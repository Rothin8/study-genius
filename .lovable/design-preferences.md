# Solution.AI visual direction

- Bright white and soft gray surfaces with cobalt blue primary accents and near-black text.
- Space Grotesk headings and DM Sans body text.
- Full-width story layout inspired by graph paper and academic notebooks.
- Academic Cobalt direction: crisp borders, restrained shadows, compact radii, clear hierarchy.
- Avoid dark glassmorphism and generic sparkle-based AI identity.
